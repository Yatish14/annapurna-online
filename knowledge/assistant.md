# Annapurna Assist Guide

This guide explains how to use Annapurna Assist, the AI helper in the dashboard: what it can look up, how its drafts and the Confirm button work, saved chats, the daily question limit, and asking by voice.

## Opening Annapurna Assist

There are two ways to open Annapurna Assist:

- Press **Ask Assist** on any dashboard page. The chat opens in a panel on the right, beside the page.
- Click **Annapurna Assist** at the top of the sidebar to open the full page. From the side panel, the **Open the full page** button does the same.

Chats stay open while you move between pages. Press **New chat** to start a fresh conversation. An empty chat shows a few example questions that you can tap to ask.

## What Annapurna Assist can look up

Annapurna Assist answers from the dashboard's own data. It can:

- List bookings by dates or status, find a booking by customer, driver, vehicle, phone or booking number, and show one booking's details.
- Tell you who still owes money, and what is owed to drivers.
- Give a month's overview (money in, money out, net) or a month's report with its PDF.
- Show how each vehicle and driver is doing, and list printout orders.
- Get a booking's expense statement PDF.
- Explain how to use the dashboard.

Tables, PDFs and links appear as cards under the answer. Questions about anything else, such as general knowledge or coding, are politely declined.

## Drafts and the Confirm button

Annapurna Assist never saves anything by itself. It can prepare three kinds of drafts:

- a **new booking**,
- a **payment** (from a customer, or to a driver),
- a **fuel** entry (on a booking, or on a vehicle).

It checks each draft with the dashboard form's rules. If something is missing or unclear, such as an amount, a date, or which of two drivers you mean, it asks you. When the draft is ready, a card shows the details with three buttons:

- **Confirm** saves it.
- **Open in form** (for a booking) or **Open the booking** / **Open the vehicle** (for a payment or fuel) opens the dashboard page to check or change it there.
- **Cancel** throws the draft away. Nothing is saved.

## What happens when you press Confirm

When you press **Confirm** on a draft card, the server checks everything again, because things may have changed since the draft was made. If it passes, the booking, payment or fuel entry is saved exactly as if it had been typed into the form, and the activity log notes it was made via Annapurna Assist. A new booking card then shows its booking number and **Open the booking**. A draft is saved only once, even if Confirm is pressed twice. A draft can be confirmed for 24 hours; after that it shows **Expired** and you can ask again. Only the admin who asked can confirm a draft.

## Saved chats

Your chats are saved, so you can come back to them. Press **Your chats** to see the list, grouped by Today, Yesterday and older dates. Each chat is named automatically from its first question. Your chats are private: other users can't see them.

- **Rename a chat**: press the **Rename chat** (pencil) button, type the new name, and press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.
- **Delete a chat**: press the **Delete chat** (trash) button and confirm. The chat and all its messages are gone for good.
- **Search chats**: type in **Search chats**. It searches chat titles and the words inside messages.

In a very long chat, the oldest questions and answers are left out of what the assistant reads, so it may ask you to repeat something from much earlier.

## Daily question limit

Each admin can ask **50 questions per day**, so that one admin can't use up the assistant's shared daily allowance. The count starts again at midnight, India time. The chat shows how many questions are left today; when none are left, it says "No questions left today. The limit resets at midnight." A question can be up to 1,000 characters. While an answer is being written, the **Stop** button stops it.

## Annapurna Assist for viewers

Every role can open Annapurna Assist, from the sidebar or the **Ask Assist** button, but for viewers it is view only. Viewers can look around, but the question box, the microphone, the send button, **New chat** and the draft buttons are greyed out. Pressing one explains that asking questions and confirming drafts is for admins. A viewer who needs to ask questions should ask the super admin for access.

## Languages and asking by voice

You can ask in English, Telugu or Hindi; Annapurna Assist replies in the language the question is written in. To ask by voice, press the microphone button in the question box and speak. The words appear in the box as you talk, and it stops by itself after a pause. Check or correct the text, then press **Send**.

To choose the language you will speak, press the language button next to the mic (it shows **EN**, **TE** or **HI**) and pick English, Telugu or Hindi under "I'll speak in". The mic listens for Telugu unless you pick another language.

## When voice doesn't work

Voice needs:

- **Chrome or Edge**. Firefox and Brave don't support it.
- A secure page (an address starting with https://).
- Permission to use the microphone. If it is blocked, click the icon at the left of the address bar and allow Microphone.
- An internet connection. Chrome sends your voice to Google to turn it into text, and some office networks or VPNs block that.

If voice can't work, the app shows a message saying why. You can always type the question instead.
