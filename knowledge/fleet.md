# Vehicles and Drivers Guide

This guide explains the **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers** page: adding, editing, switching off and deleting vehicles and drivers, and what each vehicle's and driver's page shows.

## The Vehicles & drivers page

**Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers** lists the cars and drivers that can be picked when creating a booking. It has two panels, **Vehicles** and **Drivers**, each with a form to add one and the number in use. Each row shows a status:

- **Available**: in use and not on a trip today.
- **On trip · VB-0042**: on that booking today (click it to open the booking).
- **Switched off**: can't be picked for new bookings.

Vehicle rows also show the number of bookings, km, fuel and repairs. Driver rows show the mobile number, bookings and any amount still to pay. Click a row to open its page.

## Adding a vehicle

1. Open **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers**.
2. In the **Vehicles** panel, type the name in **New vehicle (car)**, for example a model and colour.
3. Press **Add vehicle**.

The name must be 2 to 60 letters, and two vehicles can't have the same name. Only cars can be added for now. A vehicle can also be added from the booking form with **+ New vehicle**; it is then chosen for that booking. Loan and insurance details can be added later on the vehicle's page.

## Adding a driver

1. Open **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers**.
2. In the **Drivers** panel, fill in **Driver name** and **Mobile number**.
3. Press **Add driver**.

The name must be 2 to 60 letters, and the mobile number must be a valid 10-digit number. Two drivers can't have the same mobile number. A driver can also be added from the booking form with **+ New driver**.

## A vehicle's page

Open a vehicle from **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers** to see its page. The tiles at the top show **Bookings**, **Km travelled**, **Fuel**, **Tolls & other**, **Repairs**, **FASTag**, **EMI paid** and **Insurance paid**. Below them are the **Loan / EMI** and **Insurance** panels, then **Fuel** (from its bookings and added directly), **Repairs & servicing**, **FASTag recharges** and **Latest bookings**. Costs can be added straight from these panels. To rename the vehicle, press **Edit name** in the page header, type the new name and save.

## A driver's page

Open a driver from **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers** to see their page. The tiles show **Bookings**, **Days driven**, **Km driven**, **Earned** (the agreed driver amounts), **Paid** and **Still to pay**. Below are a **Still to pay** list of bookings not settled, the driver's payments, and their **Bookings**. The page can show **All time**, a **Month** (with arrows to step through months) or **Dates** from–to: bookings that start in that time and payments made in it. To change the name or mobile number, press **Edit name or mobile** in the header.

## Switching off or deleting a vehicle or driver

A vehicle or driver that already has bookings can't be deleted, only switched off. To switch one off, open its page and press **Switch off**, then confirm. It disappears from the lists for new bookings, but all its history stays. Bookings that already use it can keep it when edited. To use it again, open its page and press **Switch on**.

A vehicle or driver that has never been used on a booking can be deleted instead. Open its page and press **Delete**, then confirm. This can't be undone. If the **Delete** button isn't there, the vehicle or driver has bookings; use **Switch off** instead.

## Vehicle loans and insurance

Each vehicle's page has a **Loan / EMI** panel and an **Insurance** panel. They are the same panels as on **Expense Tracker → EMI & insurance**, which shows every vehicle's loan and policy in one place with reminders. Use **Add loan details** and **Mark EMI paid** for EMIs, and **Add insurance** and **Renew policy** for insurance. See the fuel and expenses guide for the steps.
