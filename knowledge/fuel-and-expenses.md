# Fuel and Expenses Guide

This guide explains how to record fuel, other trip expenses, repairs, FASTag recharges, EMI and insurance in the Expense Tracker, and where each one counts.

## Booking costs and vehicle costs

The Expense Tracker splits costs into two kinds:

- **Booking costs** belong to one booking: fuel filled for the trip and **other expenses** such as tolls, parking, permits or the driver's food. They count in that booking's profit.
- **Vehicle costs** belong to a vehicle, not a booking: **repairs and servicing**, **FASTag recharges**, **EMI** and **insurance**. They count in the vehicle's monthly profit, in Money out on the Overview and in the Reports, but never in a single booking's profit.

Fuel can also be added straight to a vehicle when it wasn't for a booking.

## Recording fuel on a booking

1. Open the booking from **Expense Tracker → Bookings**.
2. In the **Fuel** panel, press **Add fuel**.
3. Enter the **Amount (₹)**.
4. Optionally enter **Litres** (for example 35.5).
5. Check the **Date** and optionally add a **Note**, such as "Full tank".
6. Press **Save fuel**.

The fuel goes on the booking's vehicle too. Fuel can also be entered on the **New booking** form (**Fuel amount** and **Litres**) or added with **Edit details** (**Add fuel**). Fuel can't be added to a cancelled booking.

## Recording fuel not for a booking

To record fuel filled when the vehicle wasn't on a booking, open **Expense Tracker → Vehicles & drivers**, open the vehicle, and press **Add fuel** in its **Fuel** panel. Enter the amount, optional litres, date and note, then press **Save fuel**. The vehicle's Fuel panel shows both kinds of fuel: fuel filled on its bookings (with the booking number) and fuel added to the vehicle directly.

## Fuel entry rules

- The amount must be more than zero, in rupees with up to two decimals, and at most ₹1 crore.
- Litres are optional, but must be a number up to 9,999 if entered. Litres without an amount are refused.
- The date must be a valid date.
- On a new booking, fuel is dated the start day if the trip has already started, otherwise today. Fuel added in the edit form is dated today.

## Recording other expenses on a booking

Other expenses are trip costs other than fuel. To add one:

1. Open the booking and find the **Other expenses** panel.
2. Press **Add expense**.
3. Under **Expense**, choose a name from the list or type a new one. The list has Toll, Parking, State permit / entry tax, Driver food, Driver room, Car wash and Fine / challan, plus names typed on earlier bookings.
4. Enter the **Amount (₹)**, the **Date** and an optional **Note**.
5. Press **Save expense**.

Other expenses can only be added on a booking, not straight to a vehicle.

## Recording a repair or service

Repairs and servicing belong to the vehicle. To add one:

1. Open **Expense Tracker → Repairs** (or the vehicle's page from **Vehicles & drivers**).
2. Press **Add repair**.
3. On the Repairs page, choose the **Vehicle**.
4. Fill in **What was done** (for example "Engine oil change"), the **Amount (₹)** and the **Date**.
5. Optionally add the **Showroom or shop name**, the shop's state and city, and a **Note**.
6. Press **Save repair**.

The **Repairs** page shows one month at a time. Use ‹ and › to move between months, and the vehicle buttons (or **All vehicles**) to filter. It shows the month's total, the number of repairs, and each vehicle's total under **By vehicle**.

## Recording a FASTag recharge

1. Open **Expense Tracker → FASTag** (or the vehicle's page).
2. Press **Add recharge**.
3. On the FASTag page, choose the **Vehicle**.
4. Enter the **Amount (₹)** and the **Recharged on** date.
5. Optionally enter the **FASTag bank or app** and a **Note**.
6. Press **Save FASTag recharge**.

Like Repairs, the **FASTag** page shows one month at a time, can be filtered by vehicle, and shows each vehicle's total.

## Recording an EMI payment

EMI is tracked on **Expense Tracker → EMI & insurance** and on each vehicle's page. First add the loan with **Add loan details**: **Bank or finance company**, **EMI per month (₹)**, **Due on day of the month** (1 to 31), **First EMI month** and **Last EMI month**, then **Save loan details**.

To record a month's EMI, press **Mark EMI paid**, check **EMI for the month**, **Amount (₹)** and **Paid on** (not in the future), add an optional note, and press **Save EMI payment**. Only one EMI can be recorded per month; remove the wrong entry first to record it again. Each month shows Paid, Due, Overdue, Not started or Completed.

## Recording an insurance premium

Insurance is on **Expense Tracker → EMI & insurance** and each vehicle's page. Press **Add insurance** and fill in **Insurance company**, **Policy number** (optional), **Premium (₹)**, **Valid from** and **Valid to**. To count the premium as a cost, tick **Also add the premium to this vehicle's costs, paid on** and choose the date. Press **Save insurance**.

When a policy ends, press **Renew policy**. It fills in the next year's dates, asks for the new policy number and **Premium paid on**, and **Save renewal** records the premium as paid. Reminders appear for insurance that has expired or expires within 30 days.

## Removing a cost entered by mistake

A fuel entry, other expense, repair, FASTag recharge, EMI payment or premium entered by mistake can be removed with the trash (Remove) button next to it. After you confirm, it stays in the list crossed out, with who removed it and when, and it no longer counts in any total. Removing loan or insurance details keeps the payments already recorded.
