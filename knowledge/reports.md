# Overview, Reports and Statements Guide

This guide explains the **Expense Tracker → Overview** page, the monthly **Reports** and their PDFs, the customer expense statement, and how money in, net and profit differ.

## The Overview page

**Expense Tracker → Overview** shows one month at a time. Use ‹ and › next to the month name to move between months; **Back to this month** returns to the current month. The page shows:

- Four tiles: **Money in**, **Money out**, **Net** and **Bookings** (with km and the amount billed).
- **Reminders** for unpaid EMIs this month and insurance that has expired or expires within 30 days.
- **To collect from customers** and **To pay drivers**, over all months.
- **Vehicles in** the month: each vehicle's bookings, billed, received, driver, fuel, other, repairs, FASTag, EMI, insurance and profit.
- **Customers who still owe**, **Drivers to pay**, **Out on a trip today** and **Coming up (next 14 days)**.

## Money in, money out and net (cash)

The Overview's top tiles count cash that actually moved during the month:

- **Money in** is every customer payment made in the month, whatever booking it was for.
- **Money out** is everything paid out in the month: payments to drivers, fuel, tolls and other expenses, repairs, FASTag, EMI and insurance.
- **Net** is money in minus money out.

Net is a cash figure. It is not the same as profit, because a payment received this month may be for a booking that started last month.

## How profit is worked out

Profit is counted per booking, on money actually received:

- **Booking profit** = received from the customer − driver amount − fuel − other expenses. The driver amount is the agreed one, even before it is paid.
- While a balance is still due, the booking's page also shows the profit **once fully paid** (the total instead of received).
- **Vehicle profit for a month** = received for bookings starting in the month − their driver amounts − their fuel and other expenses − the vehicle's repairs, FASTag, EMI and insurance paid in the month.

Repairs, FASTag, EMI and insurance never reduce a single booking's profit; they reduce the vehicle's and the month's profit.

## Opening a monthly report

1. Open **Expense Tracker → Reports**.
2. Choose the month with ‹ and ›, or pick it in the month box and press **Show**.
3. Choose a tab: **All**, **Bookings**, **Customers**, **Drivers** or **Vehicles**.
4. Press **Download PDF** to save that report, or on the All tab, **Download all as PDF**.

The PDFs are landscape A4. In the All PDF, each report starts on a new page. Every PDF download is recorded in Activity.

## The Bookings report

The **Bookings** report lists every booking starting in the month with where it went (the city), total, received, balance, driver pay, fuel, other expenses and profit. Its tiles show **Bookings**, **Billed**, **Received**, **Balance due**, **Driver pay**, **Fuel**, **Other expenses** and **Bookings profit** (with the profit once fully paid, if different). It then shows the month's vehicle costs, **Repairs**, **FASTag** and **EMI & insurance**, and the **Total profit**: bookings profit minus those vehicle costs, "after every cost".

## The Customers report

The **Customers** report groups the month's bookings by customer, matched by mobile number. For each customer it shows their bookings in the month, what they were billed, what they have paid and what they still owe, with totals. The tiles show **Customers**, **Billed**, **Paid** and **Still to pay us**. Use it to see who owes money for a month's trips.

## The Drivers report

The **Drivers** report shows, for each driver, their bookings and days in the month, the pay agreed, what we have paid them and what we still have to pay, with totals. The tiles show **Drivers**, **Pay agreed**, **Paid** and **Still to pay**. Pay agreed is the sum of the driver amounts on their bookings. Use it to settle drivers at the end of a month; payments are still recorded one booking at a time, on each booking's page. A single driver's page under **Vehicles & drivers** shows the same figures for any month or dates.

## The Vehicles report

The **Vehicles** report shows each vehicle's bookings, km, billed, received, driver pay, fuel, other expenses, repairs, FASTag, EMI, insurance and profit for the month. It also lists every repair, FASTag recharge, EMI and insurance payment of the month one by one. The vehicles' total profit equals the Bookings report's total profit. Use it to see which vehicle earned the most, or which one cost the most to keep running, in a month.

## The customer expense statement

The expense statement is a one-page PDF to send a customer. To get it, open the booking and press **Expense statement (PDF)**. It appears once the total is entered and the booking is not cancelled. It comes from Annapurna Tours & Travels and shows the total, each payment received (date and how it was paid) and the balance to pay, or "Fully paid". It shows only amounts: no vehicle, driver, route, payment notes, phone number or email. It is called an expense statement, never an invoice or bill, because the business has no GST registration. Anyone signed in can download it, and each download is recorded in Activity.

## The Activity pages

Each module has an **Activity** page in the sidebar: **Printout → Activity** and **Expense Tracker → Activity**. They show who did what and when: bookings, payments, fuel and repair entries, vehicle and driver changes, PDF downloads, team changes and removals. A booking's page also has a **History** panel with everything done on that booking. Activity is for admins and the super admin only; viewers see the menu item greyed out.
